Privacy Policy

This page is run by BoatCarpet.com (KTM Interiors Inc.) for a private recreational paddle tennis group.

When you add your name, email or phone number on the Wednesday Paddle sign-up page, that information is used only to send you weekly game invites. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your email and phone number are stored privately and are not visible to other players on the page.

You can remove yourself from the list at any time by tapping "remove from list" next to your name on the sign-up page, or by replying STOP to any text message.
