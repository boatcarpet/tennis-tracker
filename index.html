How this works:
  1. Add your name and email once. You stay on the list.
  2. An email goes out every Thursday at 8am, the morning after paddle. That's when sign-up opens for the next Wednesday. A reminder follows Sunday at 2pm.
  3. Tap IN on your name. Courts fill 4 at a time, in the order people say IN.
  4. A second email goes out Tuesday at 2pm with the courts.
If the count isn't a multiple of 4, the extra players wait until that court fills. Can't make it? Tap OUT right away — the next person in line takes your spot, and the players affected get an email. If you come back in, you go to the back of the line.
